# Owner Guide — How to Manage Your Shop Website

Welcome! Your website is already live. This short guide shows you how to update it yourself. You do **not** need any technical knowledge.

---

## 1. Login

1. Open your website URL and add `/owner`  
   Example: `https://raj-tailors.onrender.com/owner/login.html`
2. Enter the **Username** and **Password** given to you.
3. Click **Login**.

You will see the **Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Dashboard

Here you can see:

- How many enquiries you have
- How many services and photos
- Whether the shop is currently Open or Closed
- Quick buttons to edit different parts of the website
- **Website Preview** — switch between Mobile, Tablet and Desktop to see how customers view your site on different devices

---

## 3. What You Can Change

### About & Hero (✏️ About)
- Main heading and subtitle on the top banner
- Hero / banner photo
- Shop story (About Us text)
- Your photo
- Experience and speciality
- “Why Choose Us” points

### Services (✂️ Services)
- Add, edit or delete services
- Set name, price, short description and emoji icon
- Click **Save All** after changes

### Gallery (📸 Gallery)
- Upload photos of your work
- Choose a category (Men’s, Women’s, Wedding, Suit, etc.)
- Delete old photos

### Contact (📍 Contact)
- Shop name, your name
- Phone and WhatsApp number
- Address and pincode
- Google Maps link
- Google Reviews link
- Instagram / Facebook / YouTube
- Logo

### Timings (🕐 Timings)
- Set opening and closing time for each day
- Mark a day as Closed
- **Temporary Holiday**: turn on when the shop is closed for Diwali, personal leave, etc.  
  Enter reason and the date you reopen. The website will automatically show “Temporarily Closed”.

### Themes (🎨 Themes)
- Choose Normal, Dark or Premium look
- Or pick custom colours
- Optional **Festival theme** (Diwali, Holi, Ganesh, Christmas, New Year, Independence Day, Republic Day) with start and end dates
- Enable/disable languages: English, Marathi (मराठी), Hindi (हिन्दी)
- Set **default language** for new visitors (English / Marathi / Hindi)
- Customers can still switch language themselves; their choice is remembered
- Show or hide the Open/Closed badge

### Enquiries (📨 Enquiries)
- See all customer enquiries
- Search by name or mobile
- Filter by date
- **Download PDF** or **Download Excel** (select rows first if you want only some)
- Delete individual enquiries

> Enquiries are kept for **7 days** only.  
> Download PDF/Excel if you want to keep a permanent record.

### Settings (⚙️ Settings)
- Change your login password

---

## 4. View Your Live Website

Click **🌐 View Website** in the menu (opens in a new tab) or just open your main URL.

After you save changes, refresh the public website to see them.

---

## 5. Logout

Click **🚪 Logout** when you finish.

---

## Tips

- Always click **Save** after editing.
- Use clear, good-quality photos (phone photos are fine).
- Keep WhatsApp number with country code, example: `+919876543210`.
- For Google Reviews and Maps, open Google Maps → your shop → Share → copy the link.
- If you forget your password, contact the person who created your website.

---

Thank you for using the platform. Your customers will love the professional website!
